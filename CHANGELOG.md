## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-16

First public release: implementation, tests, committed results, figures and the three
design documents.

### What it does

`agent_gate` labels every value with an information-flow label, propagates it through every derivation, and refuses a tool call before it runs when the join of its arguments may not reach that sink.

### Included

- Source under `src/agent_gate/`, standard library only except where
  `pyproject.toml` declares otherwise.
- Unit tests, runnable with `PYTHONPATH=src` and no third-party test runner.
- `results/results.json` and the figures, regenerated by `make eval`.
- `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`, `docs/EVALUATION.md`.
- `scripts/check_repro.py`, which re-runs the evaluation and fails if the committed
  table no longer matches.

### Known limits

See Limitations in the README. Each one is measured or argued in `docs/`, not left
for a reader to discover.
